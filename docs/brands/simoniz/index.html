Simoniz

Simoniz makes car cleaning and care products: shampoos, waxes and polishes, glass, wheel and trim cleaners, and interior care.

About the brand

  • Founded in 1910. By the 1930s, “to Simoniz” had become a verb for polishing a car to a high shine.
  • Today its products are developed in Manchester, England.
